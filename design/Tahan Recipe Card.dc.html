Tamales de Nochebuena

Makes 24
3 hrs · plus soaking
Cooked 14 times

You need

2 lbmasa harina

1 cuplard, whipped

3 cupswarm broth

2 tspsalt

3 lbpork shoulder

6guajillo chiles

3garlic cloves

1 pkgcorn husks

Then

1Soak the husks in hot water until soft, an hour.

2Simmer the pork with garlic until it shreds, 2 hrs. Keep the broth.

3Toast and soak the chiles, blend smooth, cook into the pork.

4Whip the lard light; work in masa, broth, salt. Spreads like frosting.

5Spread masa on each husk, fill, fold, stand upright in the steamer.

6Steam 90 minutes. Done when the husk peels away clean.

In Rosa's words

My mother made these the night before Christmas with whoever was in the kitchen, and the point was never the tamales. Somebody spreads, somebody fills, somebody argues about how much chile. If you are doing it alone you have missed it — call one of the children in.

Notes from the village

Nina — the masa needs more broth than you think. Keep going.

Mateo — froze a dozen raw, steamed them in March, still good.

Dad — double the chile. She won't say it but she means it.
